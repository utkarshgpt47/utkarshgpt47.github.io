My Approach to Analytics Consulting

I believe great analytics is not about fancy tools. It's about asking the right questions and building systems that deliver answers reliably. Here's how I approach every analytics engagement:

1. Understand the Business First

Before touching any data, I invest time understanding your business model, competitive landscape, and the specific decisions you need data to inform. Analytics without business context is just numbers.

2. Audit Your Data Ecosystem

I assess your current data sources, quality, and infrastructure. Where does your data live? Is it clean and reliable? Are there gaps? This audit reveals what's possible now and what infrastructure investments may be needed.

3. Define Success Metrics

Together, we define the key performance indicators (KPIs) that directly tie to business outcomes. Not vanity metrics, but metrics that, when moved, generate real value.

4. Build, Visualize, and Deliver

I build the analytics infrastructure (data pipelines, warehouses, dashboards, and models) with a focus on automation and self-service. The goal is to make insights effortless for your team to access daily.

5. Train and Empower

Great analytics solutions are only valuable when your team can use them independently. I provide documentation, training, and ongoing support to ensure adoption sticks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which BI tool is best for my business?

It depends on your team size, budget, and technical capabilities. Tableau excels at complex visual analytics; Power BI integrates well with Microsoft ecosystems; Looker is great for data-model-driven organizations. I'll assess your needs and recommend the best fit during our initial consultation.

How long does it take to build a dashboard?

A focused executive dashboard can be delivered in 1-2 weeks. A comprehensive analytics platform with multiple reports, data pipelines, and automated refreshes typically takes 4-8 weeks. Timeline depends on data readiness and scope.

Can you work with our existing data stack?

Yes, I'm experienced with virtually all modern data stacks. Whether you're on Google BigQuery, Snowflake, a legacy SQL database, or even spreadsheets, I'll work within your existing infrastructure and recommend upgrades only when the ROI clearly justifies it.
